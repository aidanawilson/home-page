ABOUT

What is Apogee Lab?

Apogee Lab is a personal collection of engineering projects, software, educational tools, and experiments built around curiosity, hands-on learning, and understanding how things work.

The goal is to take ideas beyond the concept stage: build them, model them, test them, document the design decisions, and learn from what happens next.
